Last updated: June 20, 2026

When you fill out a quote or contact form, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, move date, origin ZIP code, destination ZIP code, and how you found us.

2. How we use your information

We use the information you provide to respond to your quote request, coordinate your move, communicate with you about our services, and improve our website experience.

3. How we protect your information

We take reasonable precautions to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

4. Third-party services

We use trusted third-party services (such as email providers) to deliver notifications and confirmations. These providers are contractually obligated to handle your data securely and only for the purposes we specify.

5. Your choices

You may contact us at any time to update or delete your contact information, or to opt out of future marketing communications.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at move@wemoveondemand.com or call (561) 212-7570.
